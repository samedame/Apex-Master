# Options paper execution

The scheduled Yahoo chain is indicative. Its fetch timestamp and the 09:30 ET opening-bar reference are recorded separately. Yahoo does not supply authenticated bid/ask observation time or displayed size through this adapter. Missing, zero, crossed, excessively wide and impossible vertical markets are discarded. Option gates retain their historical fair-value meaning for same-day observations. They compare against a simplified model and cannot confer eligibility. Substituted expiries are research observations only.

For the offline timestamped interface, run `python -m apex_master_bot.option_replay snapshots.jsonl` from `apex_master_bot/`. Each JSONL record has an `entry` and optionally an `exit` and `terminal_scenarios` array. Each snapshot contains `as_of`, `underlying` (symbol, price, observed, source), and `long` and `short` objects (underlying, symbol, expiry, right C or P, strike, bid, ask, observed, source, optional bid_size and ask_size, optional multiplier default 100). Use ISO timestamps with explicit offsets and contract symbols from the source's listed chain. Feed data must come from an identified provider. A symbol-format check does not authenticate an uploaded archive. The test fixture in `tests/test_v3.py` demonstrates both entry and exit.

Fresh quotes must be at most 60 seconds old and within 15 seconds of each other and the underlying observation, all within the regular session. Both legs must share a source, underlying, expiration and option type. Invalid rows print DATA FAILURE and the command exits nonzero. The module reports bull put credit, bull call debit, bear call credit and bear put debit economics. Bearish structures remain research-only. When exit data exists, the share benchmark reports the reference-price change per share before costs.

Natural entry uses the short bid and long ask. Natural liquidation uses the long bid and short ask from a later snapshot. Midpoint is a comparison only. A limit-order fill model and implied distribution estimates require independent fill and quote data. If width is W and the natural premium is P, credit loss is `100 × (W − P)`, debit loss is `100 × P`, and the complementary payoff is the maximum gross profit. Loss adds $2.60 assumed round-trip fees and $2 adverse slippage. Profit subtracts them. Costs also shift breakeven. Quotes and costs can change between capture and a possible execution.

The expiry scenario reports intrinsic payoff, with optional user-supplied equally weighted terminal-price scenarios. Its mean and quantiles describe those inputs, without a probability or confidence claim. Existing expiry CSVs preserve their original percent-of-width marks and omit the new cost model. Early assignment, physical delivery, pin risk and after-hours exercise can create stock exposure and funding needs. These exposures are flagged and excluded from the simplified payoff. [OCC ETF option specifications](https://www.theocc.com/clearance-and-settlement/clearing/etf-options) · [OCC options disclosure](https://www.theocc.com/company-information/documents-and-archives/options-disclosure-document)

**Historical option execution is NOT VERIFIED.** The repo has no point-in-time bid/ask history to test entry, exit, implied-vs-realized distributions, fill rates or option strategy returns.

## Shadow entry and exit observations

For each distinct ticker selected by the paired shadow test, the fixed research candidate is a same-day bull call debit spread. Select the call nearest the official opening-bar reference and the next higher listed strike. If no same-day expiry is listed, record the skip. Sample entry from 09:31 up to 09:45 ET and sample the exact same symbols during the last 15 minutes of the regular session, including half-days. Record skipped and unaffordable candidates alongside both leg markets when available. An unaffordable entry can still have an indicative exit observation for research. There is no options fill or options P&L in the shadow ledger.

The default adapter logs the Eastern capture timestamp, source, option symbols, strikes, bid and ask, and last-trade timestamp when present. Yahoo does not identify bid/ask observation time or displayed size, so those fields stay null. Last-trade time is independent of quote age. Entry and exit observations are indicative and authorize zero contracts. Timestamped provider records can be validated separately with the existing offline replay interface.

The morning quote job captures entry and the watchdog provides a second opportunity during the same window. The watchdog also runs near the regular or half-day close for exit capture. Delayed jobs record a missed window without substituting a later mark. Post-close reconciliation retries missing ETF closes for the last ten shadow sessions. Scheduled jobs depend on GitHub Actions timing.
