# Options paper execution

The scheduled Yahoo chain is indicative. Its fetch timestamp and the 09:30 ET opening-bar reference are recorded separately. Yahoo does not supply authenticated bid/ask observation time or displayed size through this adapter. Missing, zero, crossed, excessively wide and impossible vertical markets are discarded. Option gates retain their historical fair-value meaning for same-day observations. They compare against a simplified model and cannot confer eligibility. Substituted expiries are research observations only.

For the offline timestamped interface, run `python -m apex_master_bot.option_replay snapshots.jsonl` from `apex_master_bot/`. Each JSONL record has an `entry` and optionally an `exit` and `terminal_scenarios` array. Each snapshot contains `as_of`, `underlying` (symbol, price, observed, source), and `long` and `short` objects (underlying, symbol, expiry, right C or P, strike, bid, ask, observed, source, optional bid_size and ask_size, optional multiplier default 100). Use ISO timestamps with explicit offsets and contract symbols from the source's listed chain. Feed data must come from an identified provider. A symbol-format check does not authenticate an uploaded archive. The test fixture in `tests/test_v3.py` demonstrates both entry and exit.

Fresh quotes must be at most 60 seconds old and within 15 seconds of each other and the underlying observation, all within the regular session. Both legs must share a source, underlying, expiration and option type. Invalid rows print DATA FAILURE and the command exits nonzero. The module reports bull put credit, bull call debit, bear call credit and bear put debit economics. Bearish structures remain research-only. When exit data exists, the share benchmark reports the reference-price change per share before costs.

Natural entry uses the short bid and long ask. Natural liquidation uses the long bid and short ask from a later snapshot. Midpoint is a comparison only. A limit-order fill model and implied distribution estimates require independent fill and quote data. If width is W and the natural premium is P, credit loss is `100 × (W − P)`, debit loss is `100 × P`, and the complementary payoff is the maximum gross profit. Loss adds $2.60 assumed round-trip fees and $2 adverse slippage. Profit subtracts them. Costs also shift breakeven. Quotes and costs can change between capture and a possible execution.

The expiry scenario reports intrinsic payoff, with optional user-supplied equally weighted terminal-price scenarios. Its mean and quantiles describe those inputs, without a probability or confidence claim. Existing expiry CSVs preserve their original percent-of-width marks and omit the new cost model. Early assignment, physical delivery, pin risk and after-hours exercise can create stock exposure and funding needs. These exposures are flagged and excluded from the simplified payoff. **Historical option execution is NOT VERIFIED.** The repo has no point-in-time bid/ask history to test entry, exit, implied-vs-realized distributions, fill rates or option strategy returns.
